## WHY THIS WEBSITE

### Problem

* There is no adjunct digital support resource for psychosocial care to aid those experiencing or caring for others who are in or out of psychiatric treatment for psychosis on the issues that matter most to care seekers and caregivers in the Global South.
* Information in these areas is subject to frequent and dynamic changes.
* Keeping information up to date requires significant investments in human, technological, and financial resources, which can be costly and administratively challenging.

<!--more-->

---

### Solution

An online adjunct care resource website with users, experts, and AI contributed content that supports patients and caregivers of patients living with psychosis in the Global South, focusing initially on INTREPID Countries: 

* Trinidad & Tobago
* India
* Nigeria

---

### Unique Value Proposition

* A comprehensive resource for psychosocial information specific to each country.
* Mechanisms that make it a self-correcting platform that ensures reliability.
* Delivers reviewed and credible information without commercial bias or advertising.
* Shortlists content according to "What Matters Most" for PWLEs and their carers.
